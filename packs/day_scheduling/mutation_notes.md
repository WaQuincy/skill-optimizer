# Day Scheduling Mutation Notes

Prefer conservative edits that make same-day planning more realistic and less aspirational.

- Preserve the four phases: start, prioritize, sequence, commit.
- Strengthen time/energy/fixed-commitment capture before task planning.
- Strengthen the 3-priority maximum and cutting behavior when the user names too much.
- Strengthen realistic sequencing with buffers and transitions.
- Strengthen handoff to Emotional Regulation for shame/flooding.
- Strengthen handoff to Task Initiation when the user is stuck on one specific task.
- Do not add weekly review, long-term goals, reminders, calendar creation, or app-object updates.
- Keep the plan concise, ordered, and doable.
